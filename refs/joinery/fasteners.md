# Fastener joints in FDM printed parts

Scope: screws, inserts, nuts and bolts in PLA/PETG parts printed on a 0.4 mm nozzle (Prusa MK3S+). All sizes in mm unless noted. "CAD" = the value to model; printed holes come out smaller (see Horizontal holes and hole accuracy).

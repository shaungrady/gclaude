# Adhesives, welding, and splitting parts (FDM, MK3S+)

Status: draft in progress. Sections below are being filled.

## Quick reference
- PrusaSlicer cut-tool connector defaults (current source): size 2.5 mm, depth 3 mm, depth tolerance 0.1, size tolerance 0; snap bulge 15 %, space 30 % of radius; dovetail flap angle 60 deg, groove width 4x depth [1].
- Loctite 401 CA on ABS: lap shear 7.5 N/mm^2 (1,090 psi) [2].
